Terms, Refunds & Privacy

Plain-language rules for using EduVerse. Last updated: October 2026.

1. What EduVerse is

EduVerse is a paid study platform for P1–S6 General Education students in Rwanda. A subscription gives you access to books, practice questions, quizzes and the Weekly Challenge for your level.

2. Subscriptions and payment

Plans and prices are listed on the Subscribe page. You pay by MTN MoMo or Airtel Money, then enter the phone number you paid from (a screenshot of the payment message is optional). Each payment is checked by a person before access is switched on, usually within a few hours.

A subscription runs for the period you paid for (2 weeks or 1 month). It does not renew automatically. When it ends, you pay again to keep access.

Submitting a fake or edited payment screenshot, or a phone number that did not make the payment, will lead to the submission being rejected and the account being suspended.

3. Refunds

Because access starts as soon as a payment is approved, we do not refund a subscription once it has been activated and the content has been available to you.

We will refund you in full if you paid twice for the same period, or if your payment was approved but we could not give you access. To ask, email us at 01eduverse@gmail.com with your registered email and the phone number you paid from.

If your payment is rejected, you have not been charged for a subscription. Email us with the phone number you paid from and we will look into it.

4. Weekly Challenge and rewards

Each level has its own Challenge. You get one attempt per Challenge, and only answers submitted before the time runs out are counted. The top three scores in each level win rewards of 5,000, 2,500 and 1,000 RWF. Ties are broken by who submitted first. Rewards are paid by mobile money after we confirm the winner's account is in good standing.

5. Using the content

Content on EduVerse is for your own study. Please do not copy, resell or share it publicly. We link to official sources such as REB where the original material belongs to someone else.

6. Your information

We collect your name, date of birth, level, email address, the phone number you paid from, and the payment screenshot if you upload one. We use them only to run your account, check payments and show your results. Payment screenshots can be seen only by the EduVerse administrator. The public leaderboard shows your first name and score only. We do not sell your information.

If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian knows you are registering and paying. To have your account and data deleted, email us from your registered address.

7. Changes

We may update these terms as EduVerse grows. The date at the top shows the latest version.
